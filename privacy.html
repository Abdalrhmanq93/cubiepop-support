Summary

Cubie Twist ("the app", "we") is a puzzle game for iPad. We don't ask you to create an account and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage data as described below. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Information stored on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, gems, boosters, collection items and settings, as well as small records used to pace ads and offers (for example, when you last saw an ad or how many free gifts you claimed today), are stored only on your iPad. We don't receive this information. You can erase it with Settings › Reset in the game or by deleting the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Cubie Twist free, the app shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

DataExamplesUsed for
Device identifiersIdentifier for Vendors; the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow trackingShowing and measuring ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention

We ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the IDFA can be used. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads may be less relevant but the game works exactly the same. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we use Google's consent message to ask for your consent where the law requires it; you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices.

We have set the ads to a maximum content rating of "G" (general audiences). For more information see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We never receive your name, payment card or billing details. The app receives a signed transaction from Apple to confirm what you bought, and uses it on your device to deliver items and to check whether Remove Ads or Cubie Club is active.

Children

Cubie Twist is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we'll help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We don't sell personal information. Apart from Google AdMob (ads) and Apple (purchases) as described above, we don't share data with third parties.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off tracking: iPad Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Change ad consent (where available): in-game Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Remove non-optional ads: buy Remove Ads or join Cubie Club.
  • Delete your game data: Settings › Reset or delete the app.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we don't hold personal information about you on our servers, most requests are handled by the controls above or directly with Google at My Ad Center.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
