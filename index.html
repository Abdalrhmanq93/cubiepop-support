Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings (gear on the map) and tap Restore Purchases, or use the link at the bottom of the Shop. Remove Ads and an active Cubie Club subscription are restored to the same Apple Account. Coins, gems and packs are used up when you receive them and can't be restored.

How do I cancel or manage Cubie Club?

Cubie Club renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel any time in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPad, or from Settings › Manage subscription in the game. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

Can I get a refund?

All payments are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do ads work?

Cubie Pop never shows ads while you're playing a level. Occasionally a short ad may appear between levels or on the map. Reward videos are always optional. Remove Ads (one-time purchase) or Cubie Club turns off all non-optional ads.

Where is my progress saved?

Your levels, stars, coins and collection are saved on your iPad. Deleting the app deletes this progress. Settings › Reset starts over from level 1.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you're in a region that requires consent (such as the EEA or the UK), open Settings › Ad privacy choices in the game. You can also turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track in your iPad's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Is there an end to the game?

New worlds open up as you play — keep popping to see what's beyond the clouds!
